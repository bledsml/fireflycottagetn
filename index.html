Welcome to Firefly Cottage

Where Smoky Mountain evenings glow.

Tucked in the trees just minutes from the Parkway, Firefly Cottage pairs a full renovation with that slow Smoky Mountain pace. Big windows pull the forest inside, the deck looks straight out over the ridgeline, and the hot tub waits for the moment the stars, and on early-summer nights the fireflies, come out.

Cook a big breakfast in the renovated kitchen, lose an afternoon to the arcade and air hockey up in the game loft, then gather around the fire pit as the light fades. Guests tell us the same thing over and over: it is close to everything and you still cannot see a single neighbor from the porch.
